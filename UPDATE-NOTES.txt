DOWNLOAD FIX (A4 PDF / sabhi download buttons)
==============================================
Problem: ek baar download hone ke baad baaki sections me download nahi chalta tha.
Wajah: jin tools me PDF banne me der lagti hai (Kisan, Passport, RTPS, Interest, SADI, ATM...) wahan PDF tayyar hone tak
browser ka "click permission" khatam ho jata tha, aur Chrome agli downloads block kar deta tha.
Fix: index.html me ek common download layer (apDlShim) lagayi gayi hai jo HAR tool/section me apne-aap inject hoti hai:
 - Har click par naya download link banta hai, isliye jitni baar button dabao utni baar download hota hai.
 - Agar tap-permission expire ho gayi ho to "फाइल तैयार है -> ⬇ डाउनलोड करें" ka chhota box aata hai; ek tap par file save.
 - Print Manager ke downloads par bhi yehi lagta hai.
Extra: SADI/RTPS PDF fail hone par cached error ab aage ke downloads nahi rokta.
Service worker cache v10 kiya gaya hai, taaki naya index.html turant load ho (app ek baar band karke kholein).
Upload: sirf index.html aur service-worker.js replace karna kaafi hai.
